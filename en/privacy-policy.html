3. AI Processing

ListifyAI uses cloud-based AI technology to process your photos. Your photos are processed through ListifyAI servers using the following AI providers:

Google AI infrastructure

Provider: Google LLC / Google Cloud (USA)

Used to analyse your product photos (product type, colour, pattern, category), to generate and edit images (background, model, pose, upscale) and to generate text. For this, the product photos you upload, the images created in the app and the requests you type are sent to Google's AI services (Vertex AI and the Gemini API).

Google Privacy Policy →

Image and video generation partners

Providers: KIE (kie.ai), PiAPI (piapi.ai), Replicate, Inc. (USA)

Used for image and video generation (model, pose, scene, video templates, lip-sync). These partners run image and video models from Google and other developers on our behalf. For this, the product photos you upload, intermediate images created in the app and your requests are sent to these providers.

KIE → · PiAPI → · Replicate →

Chat assistant and Ad Director: OpenRouter

Intermediary: OpenRouter, Inc. (USA)

Text and image requests in the chat assistant (Lifi) and the Ad Director feature are forwarded through OpenRouter to selected AI model providers (Anthropic PBC and Microsoft Azure). OpenRouter is an intermediary that passes the request on to the model provider.

OpenRouter Privacy Policy →

Voice-over: ElevenLabs

Provider: ElevenLabs Inc.

In the AI presenter (UGC) video feature, the script to be voiced is sent to ElevenLabs. Your photos are not sent to this provider.

ElevenLabs Privacy Policy →

6. Sharing of Data

Your data is not shared with third parties except in the following cases:

Service providers

  • AI processing: the AI services listed in Section 3 (only with your explicit confirmation)

These service providers maintain data protection standards consistent with the GDPR and KVKK. Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) are applied when your data is transferred abroad. Where required by law, your data may be shared with competent authorities.

8. Your Rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 and GDPR Arts. 15–22 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed
  • Request information about it if it is processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate
  • Request erasure under the conditions of KVKK Art. 7
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing
  • Not be subject to solely automated decisions

To exercise these rights, email info@listifyai.com.tr or make a request in the "Account" section of the app. Users in the EU/EEA may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, use Account > Delete account in the app. Deletion cannot be undone. Some data may be kept for the periods required by law.

10. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • All traffic is protected with SSL/TLS (256-bit) encryption
  • Secure authentication with Firebase Authentication
  • API keys are protected with Firebase Secret Manager
  • Unauthorised access is blocked with Firebase App Check
  • Access control and authorisation
