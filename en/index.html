Frequently asked

Questions answered.

Didn't find your answer? info@listifyai.com.tr

How does it work? Do I need technical skills?

No. Upload a product or garment photo, pick a tool, answer a few questions; the AI does the rest.

Does it work for menswear, kidswear and lingerie?

Yes. There are male and female model pools, a child model pool and 48 kids' scenes. Lingerie and swimwear are handled as sensitive categories with modest models in a safe generation flow; for modest wear you can choose shawl, turban and voluminous styles.

Can I upload my own video?

Yes. With Dress My Video you dress your product onto the person in the video; you can swap the model, slow down any section with Slow-Motion Moment, and recreate a template you like with your own product using Make One Like It.

Are the results photoshoot quality?

Every tool — virtual try-on, lookbook, accessories, editorial, fabric vision and video — targets studio quality; 4K output and upscaling are available.

How do I export to marketplaces?

It gives you XML / CSV / XLSX files with columns mapped for Trendyol, Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Hepsiburada, N11 and WooCommerce; you download and upload them. It is not a direct API connection.

Where are my photos stored?

Images are stored encrypted in storage tied to your account and are never used for model training.

Is it the same on iOS and Android?

Yes. All tools are identical on both platforms; generation runs on our servers, so your device's power doesn't matter.
